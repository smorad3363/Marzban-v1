## Confirmed business semantics

- **Single Admin balance for new commercial flows:** Admin's wallet is monetary Toman (`money_balance_toman`). There is **no independently debited or funded traffic-credit wallet** in the new Product-first billing design. User traffic quotas are service entitlements, **not** a second Admin balance. Keep separately configured admin limits and historical stored credit records as needed for backwards compatibility; do not delete or automatically zero existing columns/data before designing and testing a safe migration.
- **ALLOCATED_TRAFFIC / «حجم ساخته‌شده»:** on creating a customer account or reserving its future renewal, calculate the complete monetary amount from the authorized Product, chosen quota and duration, applicable Product/duration multipliers and the Admin-specific monetary per-GiB base-rate snapshot; debit exactly once from that Admin's Toman wallet **immediately** at creation/reservation. A 50-GiB package consumes its full Toman price then, **not** an additional 50 GiB from a separate Admin traffic-credit balance. A reserved period's later activation must not debit again. Pending cancellation/deletion refunds follow authoritative B clauses and must be idempotent; do not infer new refund terms here.
- **USED_TRAFFIC / «حجم مصرفی»:** no prepaid allocated-quota debit. Measure newly consumed real bytes per customer account and settle *only previously unsettled deltas* against its owning Admin's Toman wallet at the applicable snapshotted price. Retried/repeated samples must not double bill. A nominal 50-GiB user quota is not a charge for all 50 GiB at creation.
- **One-account / device-limited model:** one customer account may have 1 or 2 simultaneous device slots (not 1 or 2 distinct accounts), for 1/2/3/6 months. Eight independent fixed Owner-defined Product prices are in Toman, charged once for creation/reservation, without applying traffic/duration/Product multipliers again; `USER_CREDIT` accounts for this as one customer account, not a number of devices. Preserve legacy `SEAT_CREDIT` only for backwards compatibility where needed.
- Product-first changes must eliminate use of legacy traffic-balance debits **in new flows** without breaking legacy records, admin scope, existing quota controls, Trial or migrations. The exact old paths are to be identified in R04; no destructive conversion authorized by this decision alone. Distinguish a service package's per-User quota from an Admin credit balance.
